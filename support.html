Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

You can create an account using Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In. Simply tap the respective button on the login screen.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. Please note that this action is irreversible and will delete all your data.

How do I report inappropriate behavior?

You can block users by going to their profile and tapping the block button. You can also report users through the app's reporting feature.

How do I change my profile information?

Go to your profile and tap the edit button to update your information, photos, and preferences.

Why can't I see other users?

Make sure you've completed your profile setup and that your location services are enabled. The app shows users based on your preferences and location.
